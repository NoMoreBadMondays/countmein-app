Count Me In!

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Count Me In! is an interactive event welcome and guest check-in application operated by No More Bad Mondays.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to provide our service.

1. Information We Collect

When an organiser creates an event, Count Me In! stores information needed to operate that event, such as its name, settings and unique event identifier.

When a guest checks in, Count Me In! collects the name they choose to enter and associates it with the relevant event.

Guests do not need to create an account or provide an email address or telephone number to check in.

2. How We Use Information

We use event and guest check-in information to:

  • Display personalised welcome messages.
  • Update live attendance counters.
  • Enable organisers to manage their event welcome experience.
  • Maintain the functionality and security of the service.

We do not sell guest check-in information or use it for advertising.

3. Where Information Is Stored

Count Me In! uses Microsoft Azure cloud services to operate the application and store event and guest check-in records.

Technical information may also be processed by our hosting provider as part of operating and securing the service.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Event information and guest check-in records are currently retained until they are removed by the service operator.

If you are an event organiser or attendee and would like information relating to an event deleted, please contact support@nomorebadmondays.com .

We will review and respond to deletion requests in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

5. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers, including Microsoft Azure, where necessary to operate Count Me In!.

Guest names entered during check-in may appear on the event's welcome display and therefore be visible to people attending the event.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

To make a privacy request, email support@nomorebadmondays.com .

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed by Count Me In!. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service develops. The latest version will be published on our website with an updated revision date.
