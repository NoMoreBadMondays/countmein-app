Getting started

  1. Open Count Me In! in Microsoft PowerPoint.
  2. Select the settings icon to personalise your welcome screen.
  3. Enter your event name and welcome message, and choose colours to match your event.
  4. Create your event and use the generated guest check-in link or QR code.
  5. Display the welcome screen during your event. Guests can check in using their own devices.
  6. Watch the live attendance counter update as guests arrive.

Frequently asked questions

Do guests need to download anything?

No. Guests can open the check-in link in a web browser on their phone or another device.

Do guests need an account?

No. Guests do not need to create an account or sign in to check in.

How do I customise my welcome screen?

Open the settings panel using the settings icon. You can adjust the welcome text and colours to suit your event.

Why isn't my attendance counter updating?

First, check that your device has an internet connection and that the guest has completed the check-in process using the correct event link.

The attendance counter updates automatically, although a short delay may occur. If the problem continues, contact support.

Can I use Count Me In! for different events?

Yes. Count Me In! can be customised for conferences, meetings, celebrations, training sessions and other gatherings.

What information do guests provide?

Guests enter a name to complete their check-in. They do not need to provide an email address or telephone number.

Can I request deletion of event information?

Yes. Email support@nomorebadmondays.com with details of your request.
